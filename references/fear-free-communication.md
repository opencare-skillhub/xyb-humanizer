# Fear-Free Medical Communication

## Core rule
Do not use fear, panic, shame, catastrophe, or artificial urgency to increase attention.

The goal is not to make a medical issue sound less serious. The goal is to make seriousness understandable without manipulating the reader's emotions.

## Never do these
- Turn a risk into a certainty.
- Turn a possible complication into an imminent disaster.
- Use death, metastasis, recurrence, treatment failure, or "too late" as clickbait.
- Use phrases such as "千万不能错过", "再不处理就晚了", "后果不堪设想" unless quoting a source and clearly identifying the quote.
- Suggest that a reader is irresponsible for not taking an action that has not been medically established.
- Create urgency merely because the topic is cancer.
- Use frightening images or catastrophic metaphors as substitutes for explanation.
- Amplify a small absolute risk by hiding the denominator.
- Present a preliminary trial result as a life-saving breakthrough.

## Prefer
- "需要尽快评估" when there is a genuine time-sensitive clinical reason.
- "值得和医生讨论" when evidence is relevant but not definitive.
- "这个结果需要结合……来看" when interpretation depends on context.
- "目前证据还不足以说明……" when evidence is immature.
- Absolute risk and time frame when available.
- A concrete next step instead of an emotional warning.

## Examples

Bad:
> 胰腺癌出现这个信号，说明病情已经非常危险，千万不能忽视！

Better:
> 如果出现这个变化，值得及时和医生沟通。但单凭这一项表现，通常不能判断病情是否进展，还需要结合影像、化验和症状一起看。

Bad:
> 错过这个治疗窗口，后果可能非常严重。

Better:
> 是否适合这项治疗，主要取决于疾病状态、既往治疗和身体情况。比较重要的是尽早把这些信息整理完整，和医生讨论是否适合。

Bad:
> 这是胰腺癌患者绝对不能错过的新希望。

Better:
> 对符合条件的患者来说，这可能是一个值得进一步了解的治疗方向，但具体价值还要看临床研究结果和适用人群。

## Final check
Ask:
1. Would this sentence still be useful if all emotionally loaded words were removed?
2. Does the urgency come from evidence or from the writer's desire to capture attention?
3. Is the reader being given a concrete action or merely being frightened?
4. Could a patient mistake possibility for certainty?
