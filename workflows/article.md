# Article Workflow

Default mode: standard.

1. Read the whole article before editing.
2. Preserve facts, numbers, citations, study names, drug names, endpoints, and evidence level.
3. Audit AI patterns using zh/en references as appropriate.
4. Remove formulaic framing and unnecessary explanation.
5. Rebuild sentence rhythm and paragraph flow.
6. Apply the selected voice profile.
7. Run fear-free and medical checks.
8. Compare meaning with the source.
9. Return the revised article plus a concise audit.

Do not add a generic inspirational conclusion.
