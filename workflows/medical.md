# Medical Workflow

For medical content, factual integrity outranks stylistic improvement.

Check:
- diagnosis and pathology terms
- biomarkers and molecular findings
- treatment line and regimen
- dose and schedule
- trial phase and population
- endpoints such as OS/PFS/ORR
- guideline status
- adverse-event frequency and severity
- causal language
- certainty language
- patient-specific versus general claims

Never convert:
- association → causation
- possibility → certainty
- trial result → standard of care
- biomarker positivity → guaranteed treatment benefit
- one patient's experience → general recommendation
- promising → proven

When a source is incomplete, preserve the limitation instead of filling the gap from memory.
