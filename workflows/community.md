# Community Workflow

Optimize for patient readability without becoming patronizing.

Priority:
1. Answer the question early.
2. Explain medical terms in ordinary language.
3. Separate facts, interpretation, and personal experience.
4. Do not use fear to increase engagement.
5. Do not manufacture hope.
6. Give practical next steps only when supported.
7. Keep uncertainty visible but understandable.

For patient stories, preserve the distinction between "this happened to one person" and "this is generally expected".
